# Harbour & Finch Estate Agents

Harbour & Finch Estate Agents is a fictional estate and letting agency in Bristol, created for this demo. Any resemblance to a real company is coincidental, and none of the properties exist.

## About us
We are an independent estate and letting agency with one office on Whiteladies Road, Bristol. Our team of eight sells and lets homes across the city, from one-bedroom flats to family houses.

## Areas we cover
We cover Bristol: Clifton, Redland, Cotham, Bishopston, Horfield, Henleaze, Westbury-on-Trym, Southville, Bedminster, Totterdown, Knowle, Hotwells, Harbourside, Montpelier, St Andrews, Easton and Fishponds. We do not sell or let properties outside Bristol.

## Opening hours and contact
The office is open Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm, and Saturday, 9 am to 4 pm. We are closed on Sundays and bank holidays. Phone: 0117 496 0123. Email: hello@harbourandfinch.example.

## Viewings
Viewings are by appointment and are always accompanied by a member of our team. We offer viewings Monday to Friday from 9 am to 7 pm and on Saturday from 9 am to 4 pm. Evening viewings after 6 pm must be booked at least one day ahead. We do not hold viewings on Sundays.

## Free valuation
If you want to know what your house or flat is worth, we offer a free, no-obligation valuation for sales and lettings. A valuer visits the property for about 45 minutes and sends a written valuation within two working days. There is no charge and no obligation to instruct us.

## Selling fees
Our sole agency fee for selling a property is 1.2% of the sale price plus VAT, which is 1.44% including VAT. The minimum fee is £2,400 including VAT. The fee is payable only on completion: no sale, no fee. Professional photographs, a floor plan and listings on the major property portals are included. The sole agency agreement runs for 12 weeks.

## Energy Performance Certificate
A property must have a valid Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) before it is marketed. If you do not have one, we can arrange it for £95 including VAT.

## Landlord fees
For landlords we offer two services. Let only costs 60% of the first month's rent including VAT. Full management costs 12% of the monthly rent including VAT, plus a set-up fee of £360 including VAT. Full management includes rent collection, repairs, inspections every six months and deposit registration.

## Tenant costs
Tenants pay no agency fees. To reserve a property a tenant pays a holding deposit of one week's rent, which goes towards the first month's rent. The security deposit is five weeks' rent and is protected in a government-approved deposit scheme. Tenants need proof of identity and right to rent, and pass referencing: as a guide, household income of 30 times the monthly rent.

## Buying through us
Buyers pay us no fee. To make an offer we ask for proof of funds or a mortgage agreement in principle, and we must check your identity under money-laundering rules before a sale is agreed. We can introduce you to an independent mortgage adviser, but you are free to use your own.
