# Terracotta Lane Interiors

Terracotta Lane Interiors is a fictional interior design studio in Pune, created for this demo. Any resemblance to a real company is coincidental.

## About the studio
We are a team of six designers and a site supervisor, working from our studio on Baner Road, Pune. We design and build homes and small workspaces, from a single kitchen to a complete villa.

## Services
- Full-home interiors for 1, 2, 3 and 4 BHK apartments and villas
- Modular kitchens and wardrobes
- Single rooms: living rooms, bedrooms, kids' rooms and home offices
- Small offices, clinics and cafés up to 3,000 sq ft
We do not take on civil construction, plumbing re-routing or exterior work on their own; we coordinate them only as part of a full interior project.

## Areas we serve
We take projects across the Pune metropolitan region: Baner, Balewadi, Aundh, Pashan, Wakad, Hinjewadi, Pimple Saudagar, Kothrud, Viman Nagar, Kharadi, Hadapsar, Magarpatta, Koregaon Park, Kalyani Nagar, Wagholi, Undri, NIBM and Pimpri-Chinchwad. We do not take projects outside the Pune region.
