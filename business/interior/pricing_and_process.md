# Pricing and process

## Free first consultation
The first design consultation is free. It takes about 45 minutes, at our studio or on a video call. Bring your floor plan and photos of the space if you have them.

## Typical project costs
- Modular kitchen: from ₹2.5 lakh
- Wardrobes: from ₹1.2 lakh for two wardrobes
- 2 BHK full home: usually ₹6–10 lakh
- 3 BHK full home: usually ₹9–15 lakh
- Villas: from ₹18 lakh
The minimum project value is ₹1.5 lakh. Every quote is itemised after the site measurement, so the final cost depends on the materials and finishes you choose.

## Design-only service
If you only want the designs and drawings and will hire your own contractor, the design-only fee is ₹120 per sq ft of carpet area.

## Timelines
After you approve the final design: a modular kitchen takes 4–6 weeks, a 2 BHK full home 8–10 weeks and a 3 BHK 10–14 weeks.

## How a project works
1. Free consultation to understand your needs and budget.
2. Site measurement, free within our service area.
3. 3D designs with up to two rounds of changes.
4. Itemised quote and sign-off with a 10% booking amount.
5. Factory production and installation, with a weekly photo update.
6. Handover with a cleaning and a final walkthrough.

## Payment schedule
10% at booking, 50% when production starts and 40% before installation. We accept bank transfer and UPI.

## Materials and warranty
Kitchens and wardrobes are made from BWR (boiling-water-resistant) plywood with soft-close hardware. We give a 10-year warranty on modular kitchen and wardrobe units and a 1-year service warranty on installation work.
