# Amberwick Dental

Amberwick Dental is a fictional dental practice in Leeds, created for this demo. Any resemblance to a real practice is coincidental. Nothing here is dental or medical advice.

## About the practice
We are a private dental practice on Otley Road, Headingley, Leeds, with three dentists, two hygienists and a treatment coordinator. We offer general, cosmetic and implant dentistry for adults and children.

## Opening hours and contact
The practice is open Monday to Thursday, 8:30 am to 5:30 pm, Friday, 8:30 am to 4 pm, and Saturday, 9 am to 1 pm. We are closed on Sundays and bank holidays. Phone: 0113 496 0456. Email: hello@amberwickdental.example.

## New patients
We are accepting new private patients, and there is no waiting list. We are a private practice and do not offer NHS treatment for adults or children. A new patient appointment lasts 40 minutes and includes a full examination and any X-rays needed.

## Urgent and emergency appointments
We keep emergency appointments free every weekday morning for urgent problems, for registered and new patients. Phone the practice from 8:30 am and we will see you the same day where we can. An emergency appointment costs £85, which covers the assessment and any X-rays; treatment is charged separately. When the practice is closed, call NHS 111.

## Children
We see children of all ages. A child's examination costs £25. Children under 5 are seen free when a parent is a member of our plan.

## Nervous patients
Many of our patients are nervous about the dentist. You can book a longer first appointment at no extra cost, agree a stop signal with your dentist, and take a break at any time. We do not offer sedation.

## Getting here
The practice is on the ground floor with step-free access and an accessible toilet. There are four patient parking spaces behind the building and pay-and-display parking on Otley Road. Bus routes 1, 6 and 28 stop outside.

## Cancelling or changing an appointment
Please give us 48 hours' notice to cancel or change an appointment. A late cancellation or a missed appointment is charged at £30.

## Booking online and reminders
You can request an appointment through this website at any time and our reception team will phone you back during opening hours to confirm it. We send a text reminder two days before every appointment.
