# Fees and treatments

Amberwick Dental is fictional. These fees exist only for this demo. You always receive a written treatment plan with the exact cost before any treatment starts.

## Examinations
A new patient examination costs £65 and includes any X-rays needed. A routine examination for an existing patient costs £48. A child's examination costs £25.

## Hygienist
A 30-minute hygienist appointment costs £72. You can book a hygienist directly without seeing a dentist first.

## Fillings, crowns and root canal
A white filling costs from £120. A crown costs from £695. Root canal treatment costs from £395 for a front tooth and from £595 for a back tooth. An extraction costs from £140.

## Teeth whitening
Home teeth whitening costs £350, which includes custom-made trays and the whitening gel. Whitening is only provided after an examination, and is not available to anyone under 18.

## Teeth straightening
Clear aligners cost from £2,800 and fixed braces from £3,200. Treatment usually takes 6 to 18 months. We offer a free 20-minute consultation with our treatment coordinator to see whether you are suitable. Straightening is offered to adults and to teenagers aged 16 and over.

## Dental implants
A single dental implant with its crown costs from £2,600. We offer a free 20-minute consultation with our treatment coordinator; a full assessment with a 3D scan then costs £150, which is taken off the cost of treatment if you go ahead.

## Cosmetic treatment
Composite bonding costs from £250 per tooth and porcelain veneers from £750 per tooth. Both start with a free 20-minute consultation with our treatment coordinator.

## Membership plan
Our membership plan costs £17.50 a month by direct debit. It includes two examinations and two hygienist appointments a year, X-rays, and 10% off other treatment. There is no joining fee, and you can cancel with one month's notice.

## Paying for treatment
We accept debit and credit cards, and payment is taken on the day of each appointment. For treatment over £500 you can spread the cost over 12 months interest-free, subject to status. We do not accept cheques.
