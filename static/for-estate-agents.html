How the free pilot works

Fourteen days on your own website, with your own listings and fees. You don't pay anything during the pilot.

  1. Send your details

    Your fee guide, opening hours, branch areas and the questions your team answers most.

  2. I set it up

    I load them into the assistant and test it on your listings before it goes live.

  3. Add one line

    One line of code on your website, or I send it to whoever looks after your site.

  4. Keep it or remove it

    After 14 days you decide. If it isn't worth it, delete the line and that's the end of it.

What it won't do

It only says what your documents say, and hands anything else to your team.

No made-up answers

If the answer isn't in your listings or guides, it says so instead of guessing, and offers a call with your team.

No price guesses

It books a valuation instead of putting a figure on someone's home.

No hidden data

Leads go into a Google Sheet you own, and hot leads (sellers, landlords, ready buyers) also email you straight away.
