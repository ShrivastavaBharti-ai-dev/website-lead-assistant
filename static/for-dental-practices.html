How the free pilot works

Fourteen days on your own website, with your own fees and opening hours. You don't pay anything during the pilot.

  1. Send your details

    Your price list, opening hours, emergency arrangements and the questions reception answers most.

  2. I set it up

    I load them into the assistant and test it, including urgent and advice questions, before it goes live.

  3. Add one line

    One line of code on your website, or I send it to whoever looks after your site.

  4. Keep it or remove it

    After 14 days you decide. If it isn't worth it, delete the line and that's the end of it.

What it won't do

Clinical questions stay with your clinicians. The assistant only handles the front desk.

No dental advice

Questions about symptoms or treatment get a fixed reply that points to your team. The AI doesn't write that reply.

Urgent cases go to a person

Pain, swelling, bleeding or a broken tooth gets your phone number, NHS 111, and 999 or A&E for emergencies.

No guessing

If a question isn't covered by your documents, it says it can't answer instead of making something up.
